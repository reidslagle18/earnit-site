# Facebook + Instagram setup — the remaining steps

**Status:** the Facebook Page exists (created under Founder Studios LLC) but is
empty. Instagram doesn't exist yet. Everything you need is built and waiting.
Total time is about 35 minutes of clicking, all of it yours — account creation
and uploads have to happen from your own hands.

Use `cover-facebook-v2.png`, not the original cover. The first one was built
before we landed on the current type and tagline; v2 matches the ads.

Use the **eight posts in `brand/social/`**, not the five `post-*.png` files in
this folder. Those five were the earlier design language — italic serif on
parchment, numbered steps — and posting them now would make the grid clash with
the ads.

## Facebook Page — about 15 minutes

1. **Give yourself Full control.** Business Settings → Accounts → Pages →
   EarnIt → Assign people → your name → toggle Full control. Without this you
   can't attach the Page to an ad later.
2. **Profile photo:** `brand/profile-1024.png`
3. **Cover photo:** `brand/cover-facebook-v2.png`
4. **Username:** `@earnit.app` (fall back to `@getearnit`)
5. **Bio** — 100 character limit:
   `Screen time earned, not given. Chores and homework first, proved with a photo.`
6. **Category:** App Page → fallback Software
7. **Website:** `https://earnit-site.vercel.app`
8. **Action button:** "Learn more" → same URL
9. **Publish four posts** from `brand/social/` with the captions in
   `brand/social/CAPTIONS.md`. Start with s7 (the "5 more minutes" thread),
   s2 (First the work), s4 (emergencies), s1 (Screens follow chores).

Why the website field points at the marketing site rather than the App Store:
the site carries your Meta pixel, so profile visitors become trackable and
retargetable, and the site hands them to the App Store anyway.

## Instagram — about 20 minutes

1. **Create the account** in the Instagram app. Handle `@earnit.app` to match
   the Facebook username.
2. **Switch to Professional / Business:** Settings → Account type and tools →
   Switch to professional account. Required — a personal account can't be used
   in ads and gives you no insights.
3. **Name field** (this one is searchable, so keywords matter more than the
   brand name alone): `EarnIt — Chores & Screen Time`
4. **Bio** — 150 character limit:
   ```
   Screen time earned, not given.
   Chores and homework first, proved with a photo.
   First week free ↓
   ```
5. **Link:** `https://earnit-site.vercel.app`
6. **Profile photo:** `brand/profile-1024.png`
7. **Link it to the Page:** Business Settings → Accounts → Instagram accounts →
   Add → connect to the EarnIt Page. This is what lets one campaign run across
   both platforms.
8. **Post the same four**, then keep going two to four a week.

## What you cannot skip before running ads

The Page and the Instagram profile both need to look inhabited. Parents tap the
advertiser name to decide whether you're a real company before trusting you with
their kid's phone — an empty profile with no posts reads as a scam no matter how
good the ad is. Four posts and a cover is enough; you don't need forty.
